## Domains and optimality

Bounded variables on an atomless space permit different domains: **unrestricted L-infinity**, **all-nonnegative** and **all-nonpositive** allocations. Cash shifts must stay feasible; loss/gain signs and small-alpha notation remain substantive.

| Distinction | Consequence for interpretation |
|---|---|
| Sum-optimal / Pareto | Sum-optimality implies Pareto optimality. The unrestricted reverse equivalence cannot simply enter sign cones. Aggregate-risk minimization does not supply acceptance prices or demand. |
| Heterogeneity / mixed attitudes | Distortions differ within groups, but each is **all risk-averse or all risk-seeking**. Mixed attitudes are outside coverage. |
| Distortion / functional convexity | Concave loss distortions are the risk-averse/coherent branch; convex distortions are risk-seeking. A convex distortion is not a convex risk functional. |
| Counter-monotonic / correlation | Pairwise counter-monotonicity is not just negative Pearson correlation. Three or more nondegenerate components need special marginal/support/partition structure. |
| Feasible-set ordering | Unrestricted infimum is no larger than either constrained value. Comonotonic/counter-monotonic ordering needs preference conditions, not heterogeneity alone. |

## Independent randomization and common partitions

The imported improvement needs an extra **uniform independent of total risk**; atomlessness is insufficient. It differs from the quantile-representation uniform. Random sharing is not automatically a deterministic function of total risk, predictable trading or a feasible hedge.
