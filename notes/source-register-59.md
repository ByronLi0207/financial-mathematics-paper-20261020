## Science synthesis — 128 words

<!-- SYNTHESIS_1_START -->
Within its continuous-loss-CDF setup, Rockafellar and Uryasev minimize a threshold-plus-expected-excess objective to obtain CVaR. The left endpoint of the minimizing threshold interval gives VaR; an arbitrary minimizer need not. The portfolio setup keeps the return law independent of positions. Convex decision losses yield joint convexity, with convex feasibility needed for a convex program. Affine scenario losses and linear constraints support the finite-scenario LP. Minimizing CVaR does not generally solve minimum VaR or minimum variance. The normal-portfolio and Nikkei option-hedging illustrations report historical author comparisons of sampling and constrained hedges, not this project's results. Their optimization identities and sample solutions do not supply population estimation accuracy, statistical confidence, forecast validity or anytime guarantees. This is an established optimization and hedging predecessor; no new method or current gap is claimed.
