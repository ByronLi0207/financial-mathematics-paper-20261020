## Main judgment

This is a bounded application and model-validation working paper with modest methodological originality. The completed additions improve how seriously its empirical claim can be challenged. They do not introduce a new estimator, a new general theorem, compatible unseen-dealer replication or measured trading/funding benefit. A stronger audit and a longer manuscript are not a methodological innovation.

The defensible result is narrower than a generally superior conditional forecast: mature methods can change the ranking on the measured FX peak task, while the original Treasury regression and much of the related aggregate panel deteriorate. Several new strong baselines also lose or fail their frozen numerical gates. These outcomes strengthen the study as a controlled negative-result comparison while limiting positive transport claims.

## The corrected AR mechanism test

The fitted financial coefficients are level-AR descriptions. Differenced stationary levels have negatively correlated increments and are not themselves the originally simulated positive-increment AR model. Both old positive-increment cases and the new level/negative-increment cases are retained. The centered Gaussian population-threshold simulations are illustrative mechanism checks, not a calibrated replication of either financial source. Fixed tail starts using a stationary-marginal endpoint threshold are distinguished from conditional-endpoint oracle thresholds.

The negative-increment case with an observed rate just above nominal is within Monte Carlo uncertainty and supplies no demonstrated population violation. All tested level cases are conservative in these simulations; that does not extend the independent-symmetric theorem to all mean-reverting processes. The later elementary zero-coefficient level example shows why stability alone cannot imply a uniform guarantee over all horizons; it adds no simulated cell or adverse observation to the requested finite-horizon grid. Empirical dependent training-pool L outputs retain their adverse cells and have no uniform loss/conservativeness ordering.

## Remaining methodological and economic limits

- Both original primary Murphy curves cross. Neither dominates across the consistent-score class for the stipulated quantile; one pinball mean is only one preference-sensitive criterion.
- The declared FX primary changes history length and update rule together. Its same-history frozen controls are separate retained contrasts, not a revision of that primary. The FX evaluation record had already been inspected. Original public execution/selection chronology and later local before-run bindings do not undo earlier investigator selection. All new empirical supplements remain post-result / exploratory.
- DM/HAC, sparse POF/CC/DQ and Holm arithmetic are checkable, but their dependence, moment, exact-hit-probability and working-reference premises are additional conditions. The known floor can create atoms; sparse Treasury grids are small; adjusting p values does not validate those p values.
- C2 changes the predictor set and penalty menu jointly and repeatedly reaches the menu boundary. Its slopes are not exactly zero and it is not identical to I/F; no selection-adjusted evidence isolates conditional feature value or identifies why the original QR failed.
- The loss preference is stipulated rather than measured financing cost. Passive position-envelope accuracy does not establish a margin, funding, hedging or quoting policy benefit. Such a claim requires the relevant cash flows, risk decomposition, control actions and economic costs.
- Public code/result identities, causal fixtures and numerical certificates make the implementation more assessable. They cannot establish estimator consistency, distributional coverage or economic usefulness. Exact financial replay still requires the accepted raw FX bytes, whose redistribution permission is unverified.

## Publication-level assessment

There is insufficient basis to promise a journal tier or acceptance. A major methodological contribution is not supported by this version: its engines and inequalities are mature, and exact target-combination priority has not been established. A narrowly framed applied-risk or model-validation submission would still need an editor and reviewers to find the financial measurement use case and controlled negative findings useful enough. It should not be sold as a generally superior forecasting method.

The most consequential next evidence is compatible new financial observations and a clear decision-linked use case, followed by a sufficiently fair predeclared challenge from the relevant mature alternatives. More diagnostics on the same inspected record or another cosmetic revision cannot substitute for those. No further data/model work is silently proposed or run by this assessment.
