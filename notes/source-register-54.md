## Original synthesis — 132 words

<!-- SYNTHESIS_1_START -->
Wang and Ziegel study risk evaluation from several probability scenarios, distinguishing scenario-law invariance from monetary and coherence properties. Characterization results depend on the attainable geometry of scenario measures; general sufficient representations differ from equivalences requiring mutually singular atomless scenarios. The paper separates maximum scenario ES, integrated maximum quantiles and independently replicated maximum-loss ES. These operators have different aggregation and dependence meanings. Existing stock and portfolio illustrations use nested economic-state splits and heavily overlapping stress windows. Their estimation theory is explicitly deferred, trend-fitting chronology remains unverified here, and source plots were not reproduced. The original already supplies operator limitations and conditional-scenario examples, so they are not new counterexamples. This selected formal-body record establishes an existing financial application, without certifying full proofs, empirical forecasting validity, current regulation or a gap still open in 2026.

## Conditions and operator distinctions

| Object | Condition or interpretation | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| **Q-basedness** | Equal laws under specified scenarios identify losses; their law vectors must be jointly attainable. | Not arbitrary marginal-law tuples, coherence or monotonicity. More scenarios weaken invariance. |
| Q-monotonicity | Scenario stochastic orders differ from statewise ordering. | Measure geometry matters; no generic interchange. |
| **Theorem 3.4** | Standardness/submodularity concern the **set function composed with Q**; outer uniqueness is on Q's attainable range. | Not an arbitrary full-cube extension. General sufficient coordinate conditions differ from full-range converses. |
| **Theorem 3.8** | A supremum of Q-spectral functionals gives general sufficiency; ES-mixture iff needs **mutually singular, atomless scenarios**. | Not iff for generic uncertainty/equivalent beliefs. |
| **Lq extension** | **Finite-valuedness** retained; equivalent measures may not preserve integrability. | Dominating mixtures are not estimated true laws/confidence sets. |
| **MaxES / iMES / rMES** | Maximum scenario ES; integrated maximum quantiles; ES of maximum independently replicated losses. | Different operator orders/couplings; artificial copies are not asset dependence. |
| Properties | Coherence/additivity differ; replication may become uninformative with many scenarios. | Maxima need not preserve comonotonic additivity. |
| **Unconditional comparison** | Source examples already show MaxES need not dominate unconditional ES, even for conditional economic scenarios. | Source examples, not new counterexamples; values/level conventions uncertified. |
