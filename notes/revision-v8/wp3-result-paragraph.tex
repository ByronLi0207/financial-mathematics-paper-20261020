The exploratory level-AR/GARCH study contradicts the proposed mechanism restriction (Figure~\ref{fig:mechanism}). Both regressions achieve 2 clear gains within the proposed region and 6 outside it. These counterexamples include homoskedastic processes and the longer horizon, so the empirical concentration of gains in short-horizon FX cannot serve as a universal regime boundary. Short-history regression losses remain prominent, preserving the case for demanding historical controls while rejecting the narrower explanation. The Supplement reports the frozen design, complete grid, Monte Carlo uncertainty and failed candidate. The experiment compares the inherited regression procedures, whose menus and failure rules differ; it does not identify a pure feature-count or empirical sampling-frequency effect.
