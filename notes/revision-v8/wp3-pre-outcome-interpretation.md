# WP3 interpretation guardrails before result access

Written while the sealed run is in progress. This note neither changes the frozen support rule nor adds a computation. It records how the finite-grid results may be described after termination.

- The experiment changes history length and a specified innovation-variance persistence parameter within a level AR(1). It does not identify an empirical sampling-frequency effect. Calendar features are deterministic nuisance predictors in this DGP, without an imposed seasonal law.
- The original QR33 and four-feature procedures have different fixed menus and validation-failure rules inherited from the paper. A comparison of these full procedures is not a controlled estimate of the causal effect of reducing feature count.
- Evaluation contains overlapping peaks. Monte Carlo uncertainty uses independent repetition summaries, never the 72 within-path issue times as independent replicates.
- Complete-pair estimates can be informative about successful runs but are conditional on completion. They do not replace the planned-procedure result when a replication is missing. Missingness may depend on the generated path.
- A clear gain outside the predeclared target region must be reported as a contradiction of that restriction, even if most other cells favor historical headroom. An inconclusive screen cannot be rewritten as support.
- The finite-grid screen is descriptive. Its two-MCSE rule has no family-wide false-positive guarantee. Thirty-two fixed repetitions provide limited resolution, especially for win fractions; source values and MCSEs must remain beside estimates.
- The one-hour budget and fixed repetition count will not be revised after looking at scientific outcomes. Budget-stop rows and solver failures remain visible. A normally terminated worker can still contain failed method cells.
- Source review during execution: Morris, White and Crowther (2019), publisher Sections 5.1–5.4, on missing estimates, uncertainty and matching repetitions; no claim of a whole-paper close reading. Link: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1002/sim.8086 . This reread changes no protocol setting.
