## Citation scopes requiring explicit boundaries

Sussman's Proposition3.4 states the classical inequality for independent symmetric Banach-valued increments. Scalar absolute value is an immediate case. Strict `>q` follows from positive thresholds `q+epsilon` and continuity of probability as epsilon decreases to zero, includingq=0. This is an application of a classical result; it does not require iid increments or moments. Conditional use requires the assumptions under the conditional law.

Hoogen's original institutional indexed p59 explicitly defines a conditional quantile of a within-interval negative cumulative-position maximum, contrasting it with the endpoint. Indexed pp5,80,91 support confidentiality, daily maxima and EVT/MonteCarlo methodology. The full PDF timed out: this is partial primary reading, not absence evidence or a first-method claim.

Ehm2016 already develops elementary-score mixtures, score-dominance checks and Murphy diagrams. Wisniewski2020/2024 already combines scores with coverage and sequential calibration for signed next-hour endpoint intervals. These are substantive precedents, with the current peak response/application distinguished explicitly.
