# Source evidence for the revision draft

This map identifies the editions and actual reading scopes behind the revision's draft references. It extends the original nine-reference map without relabeling selected passages as complete papers. A full text read does not imply empirical reproduction, verification of every external theorem, or inspection of every figure pixel. Repeated visits extend scope; they are not additional distinct papers. No third-party PDF is redistributed.

## Direct source and response lineage

**Wisniewski, Lindsay and Lindsay (2020), Application of Conformal Prediction Interval Estimations to Market Makers' Net Positions.** [Official PMLR record and original](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v128/wisniewski20a.html), volume128, pages285–301. All17 physical pages have now been read in extracted text; selected original pages were also rendered and inspected. This supersedes the narrower selected-page scope in the original map, without claiming all-figure visual QA or reproduction. Sections3.2–3.3 locate the features, forecasting methods and endpoint experiment. Section3.3/physical7 explicitly includes K-nearest neighbors; Table1/physical13 reports KNN quantile/conformal/normalized-conformal variants, and physical14 reports their coverage tests. Nearest-neighbor forecasting on this same financial source is an existing precedent, not introduced by this revision. The source supports the same-data lineage and existing conditional forecasts. Its next-epoch signed response and interval scores are not benchmarks for the present absolute path maximum.

**Wisniewski, Net Position and Retail Trader Behaviour: Implications for Market Makers' Risk Management Strategies.** [Institutional original](https://pure.royalholloway.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/63018039/2024WisniewskiWJWphd.pdf),139 physical pages, SHA-256 `54856d5cc7353786c05bf411eca02529b9f70734a7dba4a3af1ebc81873943ca`. Institutional award year2024 differs from the inspected cover's2023. Actual reading includes all Chapter6 text, physical107–125/printed106–124, Chapters4 and7 text, and selected data/background passages. The whole139-page thesis was not audited. Chapter6 continues next-hour signed endpoint prediction and evaluates normalized/regularized constructions on the same source. It is neither an independent dealer record nor a journal peak-forecast benchmark.

**Net Position Time Series Dataset, version2, davidlindsay1979.** [Official record](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/davidlindsay1979/net-position-time-series-dataset). Free access is not raw redistribution permission.

## Financial mechanisms and path-extreme precedents

**Avellaneda and Stoikov (2008), High-frequency trading in a limit order book.** [DOI10.1080/14697680701381228](https://doi.org/10.1080/14697680701381228), Quantitative Finance8(3),217–224. All8 author-hosted journal-layout pages were read in text; SHA-256 `71fa9268339ae82365171caa7416833b3a5d584f05e4f4c087c4ae72d7121a8d`. Sections2.2–2.4 and3.2 supply a stated model in which inventory-sensitive quotes alter fill intensities and future inventory. This is context for distinguishing passive prediction from intervention, not evidence that this sampled client-flow proxy follows the source model or a fitted mean-reverting law.

**Boudoukh, Richardson, Stanton and Whitelaw (2004), MaxVaR: Long-Horizon Value at Risk in a Mark-to-Market Environment.** Journal of Investment Management2(3),14–19; [consulted author manuscript](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~rwhitela/papers/maxvar.pdf),9 physical pages, SHA-256 `fbd9912bb0265bfb3fc90f066ad7a48e68d72b57048c85c36de7e93df2339566`. All text was read, with selected original equation/monitoring pages rendered. The author cover is undated; final-layout equality and a DOI are unverified. The target is worst mark-to-market loss relative to initial wealth, rather than running-high drawdown or absolute dealer inventory. Its diffusion calculations are not corrections for unobserved excursions in this record.

**van den Hoogen (2017), Intraday liquidity risk estimation using transaction data: an extreme value theory approach.** [Institutional source](https://repository.tudelft.nl/file/File_3c80dabb-c431-47ac-8622-c8709d473c6f?preview=1), August2017 Delft MSc thesis. Recorded selected ranges include physical19–24,27–33,75–94,108–110 and appendix passages126–128,148–152. The160-page thesis was not fully read; confidential transaction results remain redacted. Its maximum cumulative payment-cash deficit is a financial peak/buffer precedent with different units, measurement and modeling. Neither its source transactions nor its published figures are same-target benchmarks here.

**Liao and Theodosopoulos (2005), Study on optimal timing of mark-to-market for contingent credit risk control.** [arXivmath/0506077v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0506077v1),4June2005.
