## Scientific boundaries

CFTC is an independent reporting system of related dealer-category aggregates. It is not a panel of individual dealers, a first-release vintage experiment or a common-dollar portfolio. The frozen screen yields17eligible contracts; it is never relaxed to reach20. MCS fractions describe related tasks, not pooled independent tests or future coverage. Costs use fixed coefficients and passive saved reports; no policy intervention or realized profit is evaluated. All original primary references and negative backcasts remain unchanged.
