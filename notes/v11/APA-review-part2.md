## APA and English changes available without changing numeric metadata

1. **Remove the sentence-ending period after every final DOI/URL in this scoped range.** The current entries end `\url{...}.`; APA explicitly says no period follows a DOI or URL. Keep the punctuation preceding the URL and keep the actual URL unchanged. [APA basic principles](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/basic-principles), [official APA reference-list guide](https://apastyle.apa.org/instructional-aids/creating-reference-list.pdf).
2. **Complete source elements for the two repository theses.** Current entries identify the degree and awarding institution but move directly to URL. Add `TU Delft Repository.` and `Royal Holloway Research Portal.` respectively before their existing URLs. The official APA thesis example includes the repository as source. For strict APA, the doctoral bracket can say `[Doctoral dissertation, Royal Holloway, University of London]`; this is a wording decision, not a degree/year correction. [APA published-thesis format](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/published-dissertation-references).
3. **Complete the Wisniewski 2020 edited-proceedings source.** The existing `In` form suggests a chapter, so add the five editors supplied by the official proceedings record, mark `(Eds.)`, and add `PMLR.` as publisher. Preserve 2020, volume 128 and pages 285–301. Candidate source wording, for review only: `In A. Gammerman, V. Vovk, Z. Luo, E. Smirnov, & G. Cherubin (Eds.), Proceedings of the Ninth Symposium on Conformal and Probabilistic Prediction and Applications (Proceedings of Machine Learning Research, Vol. 128, pp. 285–301). PMLR.` [Owning PMLR citation](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v128/wisniewski20a.html), [APA edited-chapter source pattern](https://apastyle.apa.org/style-grammar-guidelines/references/examples/edited-book-chapter-references).
4. **Optional narrative clarity at main-text line 55.** `Wisniewski and colleagues` is followed by both the three-author 2020 paper and the single-author 2024 thesis. Avoid implicitly assigning coauthors to the thesis: `Wisniewski et al. (2020) and Wisniewski (2024) study signed next-hour position intervals using historical states, calendar information and local prediction methods.` Keep the existing scientific scope unless the parent separately checks each source’s methods. A minimal alternative is `The related studies forecast ...` with the existing citation. The present grammar is understandable; this is clarity advice, not a factual correction.
5. **Review ordering of the two Wisniewski entries under the target journal’s APA requirements.** APA 7 §9.46 treats ordering of works sharing a first author; a single-author work normally precedes coauthored work. If strict APA ordering is required, place the 2024 single-author thesis before the 2020 coauthored paper. This changes order only, not years. The official manual table of contents confirms the governing section; a free online verbatim rule was not obtained, so this is an explicit style recommendation rather than a newly verified metadata fact. [Official APA manual, §9.46](https://apastyle.apa.org/products/publication-manual-7th-edition).

Current title capitalization is already sentence case in this range. Journal titles and volume numbers are italicized; issue numbers are parenthesized without italics, and LaTeX `--` yields en dashes for page ranges. No cosmetic case change to DOI suffixes is necessary. The whole-book citation correctly uses an edition, the dataset correctly uses a version, and Sussman correctly uses an article number rather than a fabricated page range. [APA official reference-list guide](https://apastyle.apa.org/instructional-aids/creating-reference-list.pdf).
