## What these neighbors change in our assessment

The present AR comparison is best described by its exact algorithmic construction. Rewriting a first-order level recursion as a regression of changes on the previous level yields coefficient phi−1; this elementary equivalence supplies no novel inventory model and no identification of a target position. Calling the current eight-cell exercise a strong additional conditional benchmark is defensible in its bounded menu. Calling it the strongest economically informed Treasury forecast, an identified inventory mechanism or proof of a stable adjustment law is not.

A different response is a substantive application question, but is not by itself a methodological invention. Conversely, replacing this study with a structural or causal paper would require different data, identification, outcomes and validation. It would not follow from adding a finance citation or interpreting a lower pinball average as dealer welfare. The current paper already states this boundary; these readings make the reason concrete rather than merely cautious wording.

The important next evidence axis is a carefully timed economic input, a compatible new record or a decision-linked outcome. More repetitions of the same aggregate snapshot would not create that evidence. The linked [issue-clock memo](issue-clock-target-and-known-floor.md) explains why a present-day auction/position database or a normal release lag does not by itself establish historical public availability.

## An unexecuted mature-method transfer

A future source-informed predictor could use a fixed first-order conditional mean with a small, explicitly announced supply vector z, then replay ordered historical innovations to the same declared peak response. For fixed fitted c,phi,beta, define

N_(s+1)=c+phi N_s+beta' z_(s+1)+epsilon_(s+1).

Use each historical window's own residuals and a query path z*_1,...,z*_H that is genuinely known or explicitly forecast at issue. Induction gives the elementary identity

M_(i,h)(x,z*)=N_(i+h)+phi^h(x−N_i)
             +sum_(ell=1,...,h)phi^(h−ell) beta'(z*_ell−z_(i+ell)).

At h0, set M=x explicitly. At beta=0 this reduces to the current fixed-coefficient AR replay; at equal query and historical covariate paths the extra sum vanishes. These are consistency checks of a possible construction, not a new theorem, fitted result or coverage guarantee. Historical residual blocks need not represent a new conditional future, and a fitted linear coefficient need not be structural.

Using realized future issuance amounts simply because they occur in an explanatory regression would be invalid unless the exact amount/version was already announced by the forecast issue. A calendar commitment, an announced offering amount, a realized auction allotment and a dealer's eventual acquisition are different information objects. Future redemptions similarly require the correct outstanding amounts, calls and buybacks as known at issue. Contemporaneous net financing, market volatility or central-bank holdings also need their own availability audit. No variable is admissible merely because its economic date falls before the target horizon ends.

If some future z is forecast rather than known, a plug-in point path changes the uncertainty problem. It cannot be described as conditioning on an observed future supply schedule. A stochastic covariate-path model would add another fitted model and validation problem; this memo does not silently supply one.

## Minimum design before any future execution

1. Verify an exact source and historical information history for each proposed input. Preserve first-announcement evidence and revisions separately from realized economic outcomes. Do not bypass the embargo or substitute an unverified file.
2. Choose one target/anchor/realization contract and require all predictors and completed training labels to be available at each issue/refit. Do not mix public predictors with an unavailable contemporaneous position floor.
3. Keep a small mean specification fixed; declare the supply features, scale, window, refit schedule and numerical failure policy. Specify whether supply coefficients are estimated jointly or constrained. Do not select these choices from the inspected evaluation's favorable cells.
4. Compare with the same-target historical and current AR reports under matched information, histories and origins. Preserve score, strict breach frequency/severity, floor and horizon-coherence outcomes; do not rescue a bad score by changing the preference ratio after evaluation.
5. Use fresh compatible evaluation if available. Reusing the current inspected record would be expressly exploratory, even if its new executable were frozen before the next run. A new freeze cannot erase earlier sample inspection.
6. Preserve the existing low-memory limits and retain failures/adverse outcomes. Stop before execution if announcement/vintage timing or the target floor cannot be justified.

## Decision for the current package

No new financial run follows from these references. The conditional/AR results, all56coverage reports and all original primary comparisons stay unchanged. The work remains a reproducible, modest-originality application/model-validation benchmark. These neighbors identify a concrete route for a stronger later economic model while showing why the present empirical scores do not establish that model or its policy value. A related paper's publication venue is not evidence that this different study merits the same venue.
