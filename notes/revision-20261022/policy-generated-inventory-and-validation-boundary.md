## The relevant distinction for this project

The current paper measures a passive historical response P from a source-reported N. It asks whether an issued report C predicts that recorded peak more accurately under a stipulated score. An active execution study instead evaluates actions that can change fills, inventory, cash and later information. Its inventory is generated jointly by a policy and an environment. More books or episodes are not automatically more compatible dealer/client-flow observations.

As conceptual notation only, let N^pi be the inventory resulting from policy pi in a specified market environment, and let

```math
P^\pi_{t,H}=\max_{0\le h\le H}|N^\pi_{t+h}|.
```

A forecast evaluated on one recorded measurement process does not by itself identify the distribution of P^pi after a new cap, quote or hedge rule is installed. This is not a new theorem or a claim that every policy change affects every market. It identifies the missing invariance/counterfactual assumption. A sufficiently justified exogenous-flow model, a defensible off-policy identification design, or a controlled prospective intervention could each support a specific further analysis; none has been established here.

This also distinguishes two possible later questions:

| Later question | Evidence object needed | What the current benchmark supplies |
|---|---|---|
| Forecast a peak under one fixed quoting/control policy | Issue-known state and genuinely resolved inventory labels generated under the declared policy/environment | Definitions, mature forecasting constructions and timing checks; no policy-specific labels or execution model |
| Change policy using a peak report and compare decisions | A fully specified report-to-action map, response/counterfactual model, execution/cost accounting and an appropriate evaluation contract | Realized passive score/tail comparisons; no identified response to the proposed action |

Using one score to train and a different quantity to evaluate is not automatically wrong; the objects and selection history must be explicit. Likewise, repeated observations from a held-out market or a single trained policy are not separate training-seed replications, and periodically consulting a validation day is a different custody record from an untouched final test. These distinctions must be recorded without asserting an unobserved implementation leak in the source paper.

## What a small later decision study would have to specify

A bounded extension could use fixed mature control rules rather than expensive policy training. It would first need legitimate data and a defensible state/transition model, then freeze how a report changes an action while holding other policy components fixed. The evaluation would state native currency/tick units, report and action clocks, queue/cancellation assumptions, fill accounting, fees and terminal positions. A record-count horizon could be valid, but must not be renamed a fixed duration when message intensity changes. Cost-free matching output would remain a model result under that assumption, not observed net trading profit.

The study would also need to decide whether its question is forecasting under a fixed policy or improving a policy using forecasts. Mixing these without tracking the changed inventory-generating law would obscure what failed or improved. Actual experiment size and feasibility would be checked against the standing CPU/download/memory budget before execution; the GPU training setup in the adjacent source is not an admission to this project.

No such decision experiment is added now. This follow-up supplies a concrete distinction and a future admission outline, not compatible unseen inventory validation, a new data source, a stronger current accuracy result or methodological priority. The current scientific direction remains a bounded mature-method application/model-validation benchmark. A useful simulator study could add one separate evidence axis, with its own assumptions and limited external validity; it would not erase the current aggregate-measurement and live-vintage limits.
