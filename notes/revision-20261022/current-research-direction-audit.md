## Overall direction

The coherent research product is a reproducible application and model-validation benchmark for an explicitly sampled scalar target. The question is legitimate: signed endpoint forecasts do not answer how large an intervening observed absolute position becomes, and a favorable historical construction need not survive same-target conditional alternatives. The implemented comparison now goes materially beyond the earlier one-state retrieval exercise.

The most credible finding is the conditional nature of finite rankings and their tail tradeoffs. QR improves on the inspected FX record at H6 but performs badly in the declared Treasury application; the later AR addition improves on QR in every Treasury cell. Thus the adverse QR result is family-specific. Favorable pinball differences can accompany more breach occasions, larger overrun and horizon-incoherent reports. Those are useful empirical cautions under the stated targets and loss, rather than a new economic mechanism or a general failure theorem.

The later fixed-terminal coverage diagnostic adds a useful bounded reliability check, not a new core method. All 56 matched reports and896 phases are retained; four specific QR cells cross the fixed 1120formal reference. The other 52 noncrossings are not coverage PASSes, including Treasury H24 W260 AR with59/277 breaches. A distinct integer-formula readback reconciles every exact value. Inspected-sample selection prevents treating either the individual evidence values or the familywise threshold as calibrated tests on this selected sample; no anytime or financial-safety conclusion follows. This strengthens transparent negative-result diagnosis without filling independent-data or decision-value gaps.

The current evidence is still too limited to justify an ambitious methodological or broadly generalizable financial claim. A candid benchmark can be technically reliable and still have modest originality. More exact receipts or pages improve usability and trust; they do not repair the absent compatible unseen dealer record, live vintage or observed decision costs. There is no evidence basis here for promising a particular journal tier or acceptance probability.

## What the stronger comparisons now establish

The original new conditional study preserves 72 validation candidates, 24 selections, 24 evaluated family cells, eight historical-control cells and 98 declared paired contrasts. All use their frozen labels, histories, update schedules and evaluation origins; all adverse outcomes remain. The richest models also use feature history extending before the scenario-label window, so matching completed-label pools is not a claim of identical earliest raw information or causal feature value. Menu-end selections remain documented and are not repaired by looking beyond the menu after seeing evaluation outcomes.

The later AR comparison adds a genuinely dynamic scenario construction in eight fixed cells. With fitted coefficients fixed, its identity is

```math
M_{i,h}(x)=N_{i+h}+\phi^h(x-N_i),
```

with the issue value explicit at h0 and exact unit propagation reproducing the matched S arithmetic. It changes propagation of the starting difference without supplying new residual observations. It therefore isolates a well-defined construction question, but does not isolate the value of volatility filtering or identify a true mean-reverting law. The separately fitted horizon pools are not nested scenario sets; observed crossings are a report property, not a violation of the realized targets' ordering.

AR's five favorable cells against each reference have different membership. Its improvement against QR in all four Treasury cells defeats an overly broad interpretation of the original adverse QR result. Its all-eight increase in breach occasions against S defeats interpreting lower average score as universally safer reporting. No favorable AR cell replaces a declared original primary, restores an unseen holdout or supplies a new independent replication count.

## Priorities and stopping rule for this package

The current package should preserve its narrow scientific identity and preserve the completed genuine reproduction and coverage readback while continuing source fidelity, issue finding and readable documentation. The [fresh-attempt reproduction package](../../results/revision-20261022/ar1-fresh-reproduction/README.md) closes a concrete usability problem without changing the science. Its new receipts bind the actual new execution, rather than borrowing an old PASS. The adverse crossings, floor breaches and unfavorable original primaries remain central findings.

Do not add a volatility model, wider tuning grid, new score or preferred postprocessing merely to rescue inspected results. A future conditional-scale path study would need its own fixed mean/scale/filtering, innovation pool, path-generation rule, information timing and evaluation. Full FHS changes scale and sampling as well as filtering, so it would not automatically estimate the isolated value of a volatility state. Prefer new compatible evidence for that study rather than another selection on these same records.

A stronger research-journal case would need a substantive evidence axis beyond packaging: compatible unseen validation, an identified and evaluated financial mechanism/decision, or a nontrivial new method/theory result. This audit does not assert that all must be obtained simultaneously, or that any one guarantees acceptance. The authorized current work can deliver a careful bounded benchmark while keeping those limitations explicit. The separate actual-work ledger determines when the requested approximately six hours of refinement have been fulfilled; neither this note nor a successful run substitutes for that duration.
