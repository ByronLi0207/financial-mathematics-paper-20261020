# Evidence map for the 22 manuscript references

Updated 22 October 2026. This map identifies the consulted editions, documented reading ranges and limited roles of the manuscript's references. Physical PDF pages are distinguished from printed journal pages. Selected passages or extracted text do not imply a complete visual audit, verification of every cited theorem, code audit or numerical reproduction. The ranges below summarize reading relevant to each citation; repeated visits do not create additional distinct papers. No third-party paper text or raw observations are reproduced here.

## What the evidence does and does not establish

The closest empirical work is W20 and the thesis's Chapter 6. MaxVaR, ACSS and collateral-exposure work rule out treating financial path extrema themselves as new; regression quantiles, local predictors, residual scaling and calibration have established antecedents. The present completed QR/KNN/scaled-KNN comparisons are substantive additions because they fit new reports to the **same completed peak/headroom response**, rather than borrowing published scores for a different target.

The supported contribution is a bounded application/model-validation comparison: challenge historical constructions with mature conditional procedures, match within-source histories and update schedules, retain all declared adverse cases, and qualify a separately measured aggregate application. It is not a new estimator, a first-neighbor or first-normalization application to the FX source, a coverage theorem, compatible independent-dealer replication or measured trading/funding gain.

The directed reference search and these reading records do **not** prove absence of an exact predecessor. Priority for the exact target-and-data combination is not established. Recency, number of references and source availability are not measures of popularity, methodological originality or a promised publication tier. Unexecuted methods above are research alternatives, not omitted numerical wins, and their theoretical/empirical conclusions do not become results for this study.
