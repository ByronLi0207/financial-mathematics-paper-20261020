The before-run software freeze is valuable execution evidence. It is not proof that the design was selected without earlier knowledge of the observations. Likewise, a new timestamp on an old-source replay demonstrates another computation, not another financial sample. This remains true when all exported numbers are byte-identical.

Re-fitting coefficients during a future evaluation need not violate a prospective procedure: a fully specified deployment rule may admit earlier evaluation outcomes after they genuinely mature. What must remain frozen is the rule governing those updates, transformations, tuning/selection and reporting, rather than necessarily one coefficient vector for the entire future archive. Causal updating does not make subsequent losses independent.

## Why an extra split diagnostic would not close the gap now

The existing 90 summary rows do not form one synchronous performance matrix: sources, native units, horizons and origin counts differ. Even within a common cell, a meaningful matrix needs actual paired causal forecast losses and a declared set of alternatives, not concatenated summary means. A fixed declared analysis archive also does not count every informal research choice ever considered. Duplicate/full-pool identities create ties and do not become distinct procedures simply because they have separate labels. This is not a requirement that strategy columns be independent; dependence across alternatives is part of the comparison problem.

Recombining rows or time strips of already inspected reports can describe a different selection-stability question. It does not reconstruct the original information available at every issue, correct an invalid source clock, make dependent targets iid or guarantee behavior in an unseen regime. Re-fitting a forecaster on a split containing later observations would require a distinct causal assessment. Conversely, analyzing causally generated fixed forecasts need not itself alter their issuance; the limitation is the inferential/selection claim attached to that analysis.

No PBO value is therefore appended here. This is a scoped decision, not a claim that CSCV is universally invalid or that all sample splitting is useless. A genuinely untouched chronological evaluation of a procedure fixed in advance remains a different evidence object from reuse of an inspected historical holdout.

## A concrete admission outline for a later stronger study

Before observing any new test response, a later protocol would have to fix:

1. The measurement contract: source entity/population, currency/value definition, aggregation/netting, observation clock, revision policy and available information at actual issue. A different aggregate source cannot silently substitute for compatible individual-dealer validation.
2. The forecast contract: peak/headroom, inclusion of the known floor, record versus calendar horizon, histories, features, fitting/selection menus, state updates, exact finite quantile conventions, failure rules and resource stops.
3. The selection contract: whether the object is one already-chosen family or a complete rule that chooses among families. For a tuned cross-family selector, specify the earlier validation origins and tuning rule; for a fully prespecified online selector, fix its update, label-eligibility, initialization and tie rules before the evaluation. Such an online rule need not have a separate earlier validation split. In either case, define the comparable response, unit, allowed inputs and failure handling. Current selected-family results are not a prospective test of a selector invented after inspecting them.
4. The evaluation contract: untouched origins, label-completion end dates, fixed reference/primary comparisons, fixed score/tail quantities, dependence/inference assumptions if invoked, multiplicity and stopping/reporting. A short or inaccessible test must be reported as such, not rescued by choosing a new endpoint.
5. The custody record: metadata/data eligibility checked before reading outcomes, fixed version/hashes, an actual access/inspection history and saved reports issued before their outcomes. A hash alone identifies bytes; it does not establish that nobody previously inspected them.

These are implementation questions for one coherent future study, not an invitation to install a larger model menu. They are also not a requirement to prove every population assumption from a finite dataset. Stated working assumptions, a legitimate chronology and transparent sensitivity are distinct from empirical certification of a whole distribution.

## Disposition

The current result remains a reproducible mature-method benchmark with an inspected FX sample, a separately measured aggregate application and explicitly later exploratory additions. It supplies numerical and computational evidence, and bounded realized score/tail comparisons. The stronger paths are compatible unseen information, a defensible actual release clock, a properly specified inferential question or an observed financial decision link. Another retrospective model or metric does not automatically deliver any of them, and none is promised by a cleaner manuscript.
