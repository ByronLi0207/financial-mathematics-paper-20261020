### Original synthesis — 110 words

<!-- SYNTHESIS_2_START -->
Liebrich studies bounded monetary risk sharing without requiring every participant's risk measure to be convex. Belief heterogeneity is restricted to equivalent measures with simple densities on one common finite partition and shared conditional laws within each cell. Admissibility, asymptotic acceptance-cone conditions and cross-participant finite-conjugate compatibility support attainment, beyond a common conjugate-domain intersection. Selected proofs use cellwise improvement, bounds on acceptable allocation offsets and closed acceptance-set sums. Optimal contracts are locally structured rather than necessarily globally comonotone, and cell-dependent cash transfers remain substantive. Corollary 4.7 concerns a vector of conditional laws, not ordinary unconditional law invariance. This is a formal 2024 existence framework, without estimated-belief, statistical-coverage, solver, fairness-selection or empirical-performance certification.
