## Science synthesis — 128 words

<!-- SYNTHESIS_1_START -->
Mitra relates positive excess portfolio loss to put payoffs under a common probability measure, with the strike matched to the loss quantile. This algebra does not identify a physical forecasting target from a risk-neutral option price without the measure distinction and valuation assumptions. A strike-dependent implied-volatility smile differs from tail calculations using one common terminal law. The source's empirical illustration uses a previously interpolated, historical option cross-section, not a chronological forecast evaluation. Interpolation, tail-probability estimation, illiquidity and bid-ask effects remain measurement concerns. Reported surfaces and comparisons are author results, not reproduced observations. The selected first pass does not certify broad completeness, zero-risk, arbitrage or general-measure pricing claims. This established option-implied risk predecessor supplies neither a new method nor a current gap from changing assets, dates or strike grids.
