# S2 theory and simulation evidence scope

No direct numeric MISMATCH was found against the limited theory/simulation bullet in S2 of v7. The following distinctions must survive later writing; they are evidence qualifications, not newly performed experiments.

1. **A classical corollary, not a new maximal inequality.** The fixed population bound requires independent, individually zero-symmetric future real increments and the population absolute-displacement endpoint quantile. Equal increment distributions and finite moments are unnecessary. Conditional use needs those premises and the corresponding quantile conditional on issue information. The empirical endpoint plug-in has none of those guarantees automatically. Formal statements and exact identities are mapped to immutable `notes/revision-v5-novelty/theory-source.json`, rather than the mutable current submission files. The existing Sussman source record identifies Proposition 3.4; its metadata and reading-scope record are included without asserting fresh v7 source review.

2. **True quantile coverage is an inequality.** Floor projection is pointwise pinball-nonworsening. A true lower inverse conditional quantile has strict breach probability at most alpha; equality need not hold at atoms. The floor alone does not force an atom, and a fitted report does not inherit the true-quantile statement. Exact artificial examples remain labelled artificial; they are not financial observations.

3. **Level persistence differs from increment persistence.** The archived fitted phi summaries describe an intercept-bearing recursion for position levels. The corrected illustrative level process uses stationary variance one, coefficients 0.68/0.85/0.98, stationary or fixed absolute-95th-percentile starts, and both retained horizons. Differencing a stationary positively persistent level AR produces negatively correlated changes. The separate negative-coefficient increment AR has stationary increments and nonstationary cumulative levels. These are distinct mechanisms. None identifies the actual financial data law.

4. **All mechanisms and adverse rates remain.** Original Gaussian increment-AR rows, including positive persistence, remain unchanged. The correction retains 34 threshold rows on 22 initialized path cells: 16 primary rows and 18 secondary sensitivities. Every row uses 100000 paths. All mean-reverting level rates in those saved cases are below five percent. The negative-increment H24, phi=-0.6 row has probability 0.05032; its saved excess is 0.03199999999999939 percentage points and its saved Monte Carlo SE is 0.0006912879110761304 in probability units. That finite point estimate is not a demonstrated population-bound violation. Neither the favourable level rates nor the negative-increment rates extend the independence theorem.

5. **Fixed-start marginal and conditional quantiles must not be conflated.** The stationary-marginal threshold held across level starts is not the conditional endpoint quantile at a large fixed anchor. Conditional-oracle sensitivities share paths and seeds with marginal-threshold partners; they are not independent replications. Absolute-level 95th percentile and signed-level 95th percentile are different anchors, both retained. A zero count is not zero population probability; the old readback records a single-prespecified-row binomial upper bound, without a simultaneous-family claim.

6. **Horizon crossing is a forecast-coherence observation.** All four original QR short/long comparisons retain their crossing counts and zero realized-target ordering violations. Independent H-specific fits and training pools need not be ordered merely because their true peak targets are ordered.
