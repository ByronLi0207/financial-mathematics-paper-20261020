1. **MISMATCH — the approximately 20.6:1 preference reversal is H24 legacy only.** `preference-robustness/results.json` headline comparison `fx_h24_legacy` compares QR/W2880 against rolling **S**/W720; its exact equality alpha is 86579744869851975/90776350660766843 and its existing README prints r=20.6308977263. H6 Murphy curves still cross (11 crossings); that varies elementary-score thresholds at a fixed tau, not the error-weight family. Keep these different comparisons and preference families separate.

2. **MISMATCH if “short-history QR and H24 failures” is read as universal score failure.** Original FX H6/W720 versus fixed F/W720 has a small score gain (−2.1167271486370858%) with 531 versus 332 strict breaches and raw DM p=.5379650233773263. State the specific adverse/weak configurations rather than asserting every H24 or short-history regression loses.

3. **Earlier-period evidence is a retrospective reuse of familiar history, not independent or prospective validation.** The PR3 writing-stage comparison uses the original three-menu QR (also R0), not the one-SE R2 rule. Same-window early H6/W2880 improves −16.62356533682211%, but the original rolling-F/W720 reference yields +3.1681985293173343%. Its two later HAC p-values are descriptive and unadjusted. The source explicitly does not issue HLN or an adjustment family for these two new readbacks; do not invent them.

4. **Adjustment-family version matters.** The original matched family has 72 comparisons; PR3 reports the two primaries separately and adjusts the remaining 71 matched comparisons after results. For H6 long QR versus matched fixed F, original Holm p=2.8626953049690645e−7 and revised71 Holm p=2.817965690828923e−7. The unchanged primary has negative mean differences in all three states, while group heterogeneity p=.15938333671965318 does not support a strong state difference.
