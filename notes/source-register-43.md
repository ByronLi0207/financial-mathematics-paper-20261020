## Model, existence and the dual space

The model has **two agents, bounded aggregate endowment and a standard atomless space**. Monetary utilities are concave, monotone and cash invariant; sure cash increases utility. Payoff/utility and positive-loss risk conventions need reconciliation, not merely negating one value.

Attained sup-convolution/common supergradients characterize efficient sharing under the stated law-invariance, continuity/Fatou and conjugacy structure. A finite grid does not verify it.

| Distinction | What the selected source supports |
|---|---|
| Topological dual / L1 | The full dual includes regular density and purely finitely additive components. Restricting to L1 may lose a common supporting functional in the broader class, notably with endpoint jumps. Smooth/strict-concavity settings differ. |
| Static conditioning / information | Averaging a dual object on a payoff-containing sigma-field is static measurability, not a trading filtration, estimated physical law, forecast certificate or unique market-price density. |
| Existence / universal form | Theorem 3.2 supplies **a comonotone Pareto-optimal allocation**, not all optima's form or universal uniqueness. |
| Allocation / utility | Component allocations moving with aggregate endowment differ from **comonotone-additive utility**, an additional property for explicit results. Neither law invariance nor existence implies it. |

Lemma 6.1 imports the improvement step from **Landsberger–Meilijson and Dana–Meilijson**, plus dominance preservation, to obtain a comonotone maximizing sequence. Those originals were **not read**; the external chain is not independently proved.

Cash normalization gives nondecreasing components summing to the identity on bounded endowment. Boundedness/1-Lipschitz equicontinuity support the supplied Ascoli/L-infinity-continuity route, not unrestricted unbounded-loss existence.

An atomless space can represent an atomic payoff via an auxiliary uniform. Empirical atoms do not refute that setup, while finite-state approximations do not certify continuum dual attainment or endpoint/convergence claims. No discretization was run.

## Flat regions, strictness and economic acceptance

Quantile-change, flat-quantile, equal-distortion and endpoint regions receive separate treatment. A quantile plateau differs from a linear preference-distortion segment. Component allocations must still sum to total endowment.

The AVaR special case needs **strict monotonicity and strict lower-tail conditional risk aversion of the other party**. The latter compares a nonconstant lower-tail part with its conditional-mean replacement, beyond ordinary risk aversion. Lemma 3.1 gives separate distortion restrictions.

AVaR itself does not meet the pair of strictness requirements. Proposition 3.2 permits an appropriate non-comonotone-additive second utility. Its option/layer statements and uniqueness up to cash constants are conditional special cases. Semi-deviation boundaries, entropic examples and all strike/strictness identities were not independently certified.

**Pareto efficiency differs from individual rationality.** Theorem 3.3 uses reservation-price intervals relative to initial endowments. Supporting functionals do not supply unique market prices, willingness to trade or realized profit; bargaining interpretation is not measured preference.

This is **static sharing**, not a dynamic-feasibility, causal-coupling or forecast guarantee. Non-monotone objectives and fixed-premium reinsurance need separate utility/sign/feasibility/pricing checks.
