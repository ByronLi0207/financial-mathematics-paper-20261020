### Representation, moments and conventions

The Lp functional is law invariant, convex, norm continuous and translation affine. Cash-additive risks and deviations have different cash responses. Signed Choquet representations use a penalty; the homogeneous branch has additional premises. External duality/Fatou/coupling originals unread.

The source uses **small-alpha tail-probability VaR/ES**, whereas PWW uses an increasing confidence-level quantile argument. Tail direction, left/right quantiles and endpoints must be translated rather than copying the same symbol between sources. No executable formula is adopted here.

Mean and **exact central pth moment**, p>1, are fixed. Concave distortion and conjugate-integrability support the centered-derivative/Hölder/quantile route; p=2 and linear/zero-norm cases are separate. PWW's upper budget/envelope step and LVXY's distance/shape restrictions are different premises.

Theorem 3.4 on 13 matches a later citation's label, not certified journal equivalence or every downstream implication.

### Contracts, premiums and budget conditions

| Issue | Assumptions and interpretation retained |
|---|---|

| Premium / risk | Convex insurer objective; increasing concave Wang-premium distortion/loading; convex/lower-semicontinuous/coercive penalty premises. **No cap** is not free insurance: premium stays in the objective. |
