# Primary-source reading used for the WP2 method boundary

This records actual local reading scope, not full-paper completion or popularity.

- Isaac Gibbs and Emmanuel Candès (2021), *Adaptive Conformal Inference Under Distribution Shift*, NeurIPS2021. Official proceedings PDF: https://papers.nips.cc/paper/2021/file/0d441de75945e5acbc865406fc9a2559-Paper.pdf . Root inspected §§1.1,2,4.1 in the13-page source. Immediate feedback and unprojected alpha with endpoint conventions are central to its long-run calculation. The present finite projected delayed implementation is an adaptation; it does not inherit that guarantee.
- Robert F. Engle and Simone Manganelli, *CAViaR: Conditional Autoregressive Value at Risk by Regression Quantiles*. Formal2004 publication is the bibliographic reference. Root successfully inspected §3 SAV and the optimization discussion in the July2002 author draft at https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~rengle/CAVIAR.pdf (26pages), after the formal-PDF URL timed out and a working-paper endpoint denied access. This is partial-body reading of a specified earlier edition, not a claim that the formal version was retrieved by root. The internal independent methods reviewer separately cited the formal author-hosted paper. Return-tail dynamics and the nonnegative H-record headroom response here are different targets; latest completed G, not an unavailable G_(t-1), defines this adaptation.
- Raffaella Giacomini and Halbert White (2006), *Tests of Conditional Predictive Ability*, Econometrica74(6),1545–1578. Author-hosted institutional copy: https://economia.uc3m.es/jgonzalo/teaching/PhdTimeSeries/GiacominiWhite.pdf . Root inspected the introduction and multistep conditional discussion, especially pp1556–1558, for fixed-memory procedures, matured instruments and covariance/rank conditions. WP4 retains those unverified working-reference assumptions and separates overall conditional moments from equality of volatility-group means.
