## What this search does and does not support

- A directed comparison cannot establish first or unique status for a conjunction. Joint score and coverage evaluation and known support/starting information have clear precedents.
- Wisniewski’s endpoint study already combines interval/quantile losses and coverage tests with ordered rolling train/calibration/test sets. CAViaR and the Murphy-score literature supply further joint-scoring/calibration precedents.
- The Delft original body could not be reacquired. Its known cash-peak precursor remains acknowledged in the narrative; this fresh-body comparison table excludes it rather than treating unverifiable cells as negative findings.
- The study comparison spans 13 Treasury tasks: 1 original bill task plus 12 additional related aggregate tasks. This does not mean independent dealers; the FX record is a separate measurement task, not an additional authenticated dealer replication.
- MaxVaR’s newly inspected journal-layout original labels its page range as 1–6; the old local 14–19 range is not used. Its direct path-risk conversion prevents describing endpoint-to-peak conversion generally as new.
- The Royal Holloway thesis has an award year of 2024 and a cover year of 2023. The table groups its endpoint chapter with the earlier paper; other thesis applications do not establish an independent dealer test of this peak task.
