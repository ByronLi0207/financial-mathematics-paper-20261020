# Financial Mathematics Research

An ongoing working-paper project in financial engineering and financial mathematics.

**Current stage: literature review.** The research question has not yet been fixed. No analysis results or completed manuscript are being claimed.

The research compares recent methods with established foundations, checks their assumptions and closest applications, and investigates whether a method can answer a distinct, well-defined financial question using modest public data and reproducible computations.

The workflow is literature review, research design, bounded empirical or numerical analysis, and manuscript preparation. Reading notes distinguish abstract screening, selected full-text reading, and full-paper reading. Claims of novelty and performance remain provisional until checked.

## Project contents

- `notes/`: source-linked reading notes, limitations, and comparison of candidate methods.
- `protocol/`: research question, design decisions and evaluation plan.
- `manuscript/`: manuscript sources, added after the research design.
- `outputs/`: verified tables, figures and final paper, when available.

Data access conditions and computational requirements will be documented once the dataset and method are selected. Source papers and third-party datasets are not relicensed by this repository.
