\textbf{Submission to Applied Stochastic Models in Business and Industry}\par
\textbf{Hard to Beat: Historical Rules versus Conditional Quantile Models for Peak Net-Position Limits}

Dear Editors,

Please consider this manuscript on forecasting the largest absolute dealer position reached during a review period.

The paper connects stochastic forecasting with a practical business decision: setting position limits before the review period ends. Its comparisons across hourly foreign exchange (FX) positions and weekly dealer aggregates fit your journal's applications of stochastic models in finance.

Three findings guide limit validation. Historical rules generally perform well; FX regression benefits from long histories, with gains that depend on the reference. Short training or validation histories favor historical rules in weekly comparisons; longer validation narrows median gaps, leaving large individual losses. Lower forecast scores need not reduce limit costs, since reserve and breach charges can change the preferred rule.
