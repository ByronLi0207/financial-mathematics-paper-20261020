\textbf{Submission to Applied Stochastic Models in Business and Industry}\par
\textbf{Hard to Beat: Historical Rules versus Conditional Quantile Models for Peak Net-Position Limits}

Dear Editors,

Historical rules are hard to beat: matched FX gains depend on the reference, longer validation reduces weekly median losses, and illustrative cost rankings depend on reserve and breach charges.

The manuscript addresses your journal's scope of ``stochastic modelling, data analysis and their applications in business, finance'' through matched empirical comparisons of historical rules and conditional quantile forecasts for sampled peak dealer positions. It applies existing forecasting and evaluation methods and retains the retrospective design and adverse-result qualifications.

A version of the manuscript, code and public aggregate results is openly available in the \href{https://github.com/ByronLi0207/financial-mathematics-paper-20261020/tree/submission-final}{GitHub repository}. This public availability is disclosed for editorial consideration. The manuscript specifies source-rights conditions and excluded raw inputs; author information, declarations and data-permission details require author verification before submission.
