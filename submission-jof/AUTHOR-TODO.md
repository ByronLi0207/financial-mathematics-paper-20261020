this revision. The manuscript's Limitations retains the scientific boundaries.
No journal submission, new account, new data collection or prospective run is
authorized by a local document or test PASS.

**T1 — Personal information, declarations and source rights.** Complete author
names, affiliations, corresponding contact, ORCID, funding, competing interests
and the journal-required AI/use statement. All supplied declaration fields stay
[TO BE COMPLETED BY AUTHOR]. Personally verify originality, coauthor approval,

Raw FX observations and private per-issue prediction vectors remain excluded.
Retain New York Fed attribution, modification labels and continuing source

**T2 — Independent and prospective evidence.** A single FX record, its daily
aggregation and overlapping retrospective windows are not independent dealers
or prospective validation. Related weekly categories share one reporting
system. Broader transport claims require a compatible independent dealer or a
separately registered prospective, first-release evaluation. The historical
prospective mapping remains version-specific and is not activated here.

**T3 — Weekly validation stability.** Longer outer weekly validation windows
and a prospective test of a benchmark-preserving selection rule remain future
research. The expanded menu and conservative one-SE rule do not supply that
evidence. Ordinary intercept-only quantiles and corrected-rank headroom are
different finite-sample comparators. Do not retroactively retune a failed cell
or claim that choosing a simpler candidate guarantees protection.

**T4 — Mechanism and unavailable simulation outcomes.** The two earlier
stationary designs have different menus, clocks, calibration and estimands;
PR5 already contains large short-history losses. The new constant controls
also produce large losses, so the proposed instability mechanism is
unidentified. N2 and N4 are unassessable and N3 is unsupported. Keep all old
and new failed cells, moment-qualification failures and optimization failures.
Any future study separating bundled factors or improving numerical eligibility
requires a fresh design; do not repair these outcomes by retries, changed
tolerances, successful-prefix medians or a favorable subset.

**T5 — Measurement, operating actions and value.** Fair-value net aggregates
cannot recover individual gross positions or separate valuation and reporting-composition changes from trading.
Daily last records have unknown timezone/release vintage and omit intraday
extremes; the Treasury replay uses a downloaded economic-date vintage.
Intervention can change the observed position path. Gross exposures, finer
observations, first-release records and actual decision costs would be needed
for those questions. Historical F is the default validation benchmark for the
short weekly samples, not a coverage certificate or an evaluated production
fallback. Trading/funding gains, institution-specific costs and safe operating
limits remain unevaluated.

**T6 — Public provenance and exact replay.** Decide how to supply a safe
public route for the original local design commit objects. Browser file-tree
publication does not transfer those histories. The sealed drivers and
history-dependent checks therefore require the retained original objects;
fresh-clone document compilation and aggregate checking are distinct from a
full financial replay. No unrelated private history object, machine-local
identity bundle or permission expansion is published. The original reporting
serialization failure and the separate exact-scalar report layer remain
documented; they are not a scientific rerun or an independently replicated
forecast result.
