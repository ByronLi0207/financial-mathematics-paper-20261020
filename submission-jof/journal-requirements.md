## Data availability: publisher policy linked by the journal

Research articles require a Data Availability Statement whether or not data are shared. Under the journal's **Expects Data Sharing** tier, that statement describes access or explains absence. Shared data need a repository link and citation; scripts should be publicly archived where possible. Ethical or legal restrictions may justify withholding data. This policy does not grant permission to redistribute a third party's dataset. The package must state the actual availability and restrictions of each source, rather than promise unrestricted raw-data access.
