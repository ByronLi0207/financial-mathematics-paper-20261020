## v8-wp3-qr33-loss.pdf

33-feature quantile regression: relative mean loss against matched f (%). Rows of panels show horizons H=6 and H=24; columns show level persistence φ=0.7, 0.9 and 0.98. Within each panel, rows are history length W and columns are innovation-variance persistence p=α+β. Annotations give the estimate with Monte Carlo standard error in parentheses, measured across independent repetitions. A cell is colored only when all 32 planned paired repetitions complete and its estimate and standard error are finite. Hatched NA cells show completed/planned counts; NA is neither zero nor evidence of equal performance. Conditional-on-completion values, solver failures and unattempted repetitions remain in the complete saved summary and inventory. Negative values favor regression; positive values favor historical headroom. Shading uses fixed limits in every panel; any value beyond a color limit keeps its uncapped numeric annotation. The finite grid describes these specified processes and does not establish a universal regime boundary.
