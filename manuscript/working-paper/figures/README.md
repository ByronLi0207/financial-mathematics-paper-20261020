# Original aggregate figures

These three vector PDFs are generated by `study_report.py` from the public aggregate tables only. Their exact byte sizes and SHA-256 identities are recorded in `results/run-manifest.json` at the repository root.

1. `within-cell-paired-means.pdf`: all seven declared within-cell F-minus-S means in frozen order.
2. `primary-monthly-paired-means.pdf`: all inclusive primary calendar-month means and origin counts.
3. `primary-resampling-ranges.pdf`: all four descriptive resampling ranges, the separate observed mean and replicate averages.

Money is displayed in USD millions with factor 1e-6. Negative paired differences favor F within the same objective. No range is a population confidence interval or a superiority probability. No smoothing, jitter, favorable-length selection or across-preference winner is introduced. The PDFs are original plots, without third-party source figures or per-origin traces.
