A wider penalty menu largely removes the original Treasury deterioration. At the main setting, the full regression's excess loss relative to $F$ falls from 50.8\% to 3.43\%, and its selected penalty changes from $0.001$ to $1$. Under the common menu, the full predictor procedure has 1.24\% higher loss than the parsimonious procedure (HAC p-value 0.685). The small observed difference does not establish equivalence.

The FX gain persists under the wider menu: every selection and issued report agrees with the original full specification. At the long history, changing from the parsimonious to the full predictor procedure reduces loss by 10.9\% (E3-adjusted HAC p-value $<.001$). This comparison concerns jointly selected tuning procedures and does not isolate the effect of an individual predictor.
