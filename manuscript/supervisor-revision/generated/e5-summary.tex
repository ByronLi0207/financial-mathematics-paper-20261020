

An additional panel reuses 12 previously archived New York Fed position series on the same economic-date grid, with all 48 horizon--history cells retained (Figure~\ref{fig:panel}). Expanding the full regression's menu reduces the median excess loss relative to $F$ from +92.6\% to +9.2\%, but it still loses in 34 cells, compared with 45 originally. The committed pre-run predictions yield P1: 30/45 against a threshold of 40; P2: 24/48 against a threshold of 36; P3: 33/48 against a threshold of 25. P1 and P2 are unsupported; P3 is supported. Shrinkage therefore mitigates rather than eliminates the observed weakness. The design followed inspection of the original panel results, and the series share a reporting system and overlapping dealer positions; this is an exploratory extension, not independent replication.
