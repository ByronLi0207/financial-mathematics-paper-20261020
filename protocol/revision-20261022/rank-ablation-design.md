# Ordinary empirical headroom rank ablation

22 October 2026. This is a new, explicitly post-inspection supplement. The original 22-page candidate, primary comparisons, selected QR fits and all adverse results are preserved. It is not unseen validation, a new estimator, or a replacement primary.

## Scientific contract

At each original evaluation-segment refit r, use the same W completed origins i=r-H-W+1,...,r-H and their sampled headrooms G_i=max_{0<=h<=H}|N_(i+h)|-|N_i|. Freeze their empirical quantile until the next original refit; add the actual issue floor |N_t| at every query. Evaluate the same origins and target, retaining all eight source/H/W cells. No validation, tuning, new covariates, alternative score, restricted origin subset or model reselection is introduced.

The added report I is B_t plus the duplicate-preserving lower empirical 19/20 quantile, rank ceil(19W/20), with integer arithmetic and no interpolation. Reconstruct the original matched frozen F using rank ceil(19(W+1)/20). Both capacities must be computed by ranking headrooms and then adding the same current floor; do not interchange floating operations. The existing QR summary is imported unchanged from the accepted archive, not refitted. Mean-loss QR-minus-I contrasts use the stored unrounded QR mean and newly computed I mean and are labeled differences of means, rather than independently recomputed originwise QR paired traces. Exact-origin I-minus-F contrasts can additionally be computed from the new arrays.

For W720, W2880 and W260, W*19/20 is integer. The two ranks are the lower and upper endpoints of the ordinary empirical pinball minimizer interval; both are minimizers, absent any need to assume a unique minimizer. W52 instead gives ranks50/51, and rank51 need not minimize the ordinary empirical loss. Thus this ablation investigates endpoint/rank conventions; it does not claim the corrected rule is generally incompatible with empirical pinball minimization. Ties can collapse either distinction.
