# Post-result descriptive diagnostics: stored floor binding and horizon coherence

Recorded22October2026 after the complete declared evaluation results and both source primaries were inspected and published. This is an explicitly post-result diagnostic plan, not part of the before-execution model/selection freeze or an additional confirmatory test. It does not alter any frozen forecast, model, tuning menu, origin or primary contrast.

## Questions

1. How often does a stored issued report equal its known current-magnitude floor exactly, and what are the realized strict-breach counts on those and other issues? Report all90accepted conditional/reference configurations. Also report the common response's zero-headroom count. Exact equality describes stored float64 reports; it does not identify whether an unprojected QR report was negative, exactly zero or rounded back to its floor.
2. For every45source/window/method combinations present at both H6 and H24, how often is the stored H6report greater than the stored H24report? These compare separately specified/fitted horizon procedures, sometimes with different selected parameters and mature historical origins. Report strict crossing count/rate, maximum and mean positive crossing magnitude in source-native units. Retain all45pairs, including zero crossings. Verify P6<=P24 at every common issue; a failure is an integrity error, not an empirical target violation.

## Evidence and reporting

Use only the already accepted, hash-identified private forecast archives and public phase receipts, with the executable and selected-parameter freezes reverified. Parse the exact bytes whose digest was accepted. Do not download or refit. Record input receipt/archive hashes, measured diagnostic-process resources, output fingerprints and actual counts. Do not export raw sources or per-origin arrays.

All event counts use strict stored comparisons, with no new tolerance, interpolation, dropped issue or favorable subgroup selection. Preserve method-specific breach denominators; a zero denominator is undefined with an explicit null reason. No p-value, confidence interval, independent-sample count, conditional-coverage claim or causal explanation is inferred. These new diagnostic tables supplement rather than replace the98predeclared paired comparisons.

The target order P6<=P24 does not make a model's issued C6 a known lower bound for P24. Replacing C24 by max(C24,C6), isotonic reconciliation, retuning or a horizon-switching strategy is not authorized by this descriptive plan and is not performed. The known-floor pinball projection argument applies to B=|N_t|<=P_H; it cannot automatically justify projection onto another learned forecast. The crossing diagnostic evaluates coherence of reports, not profitability or dominance of a proposed repair.
