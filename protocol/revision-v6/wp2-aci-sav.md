## Headroom SAV definition

G_i=P_i-|N_i|. For a fixed refit coefficient vector, q_i=beta0+beta1*q_(i-1)+beta2*G_(i-H). The shock is the latest completed **headroom response**, not a price return and not G_(i-1) when H>1. This lag holds in training and live-prefix recursion. Final report is |N_t|+max(0,q_t). At r fit only the original W completed G labels. Use the original positive response scale a; coefficients operate on G/a and q/a. Initialize q just before the training pool by the empirical tau quantile of up to W headroom labels already mature at that earlier issue; if none exists, NA. After fitting, propagate from the training path through r and each next issue using only G_(t-H); keep coefficients fixed until the next original refit. A new refit may reset the fitted path, as documented.

Estimate mean .95 pinball by bounded Powell optimization, beta0/beta2 in [0,100], beta1 in [0,.995], no penalty. Use six deterministic starts: beta1 in {.2,.6,.9} crossed with beta2 in {.01,.1}; beta0=max(1e-6, q_init*(1-beta1)-beta2*mean(training shock)), clipped to its bound. Options xtol=1e-7, ftol=1e-8, maxiter=1000, maxfev=3000 per start; the shared worker resource guard still governs the whole cell. Select the lowest finite objective among successful converged starts, break exact ties by start order. Retain every start's success/objective/evaluations and any bound solutions. If none converges, stop the complete cell as NA; never substitute F/QR, shorten the origin vector or retune. An exactly constant training response may use its analytically zero-loss constant fit, with that special status and no fictitious numerical convergence. Global optimality is not claimed.
