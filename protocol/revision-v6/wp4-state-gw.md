# WP4 — issue-known position-change states and conditional moments

Before-execution freeze; use common contract. Exactly the two original H6 primary comparisons: FX QR W2880 minus rolling F W720; Treasury QR W260 minus frozen F W260. No switch to a favorable matched comparison after results. This is an exploratory diagnostic of those fixed reports, not a deployed switching policy or causal state effect.

The state is the original past-only short **position-increment RMS** (24records FX,6 Treasury), not price/yield volatility. At the first evaluation refit determine two cut points from that QR cell's exact mature training-origin pool only, using linear empirical1/3 and2/3 quantiles. Freeze them for the entire evaluation. Low iff RMS<=cut1, middle iff cut1<RMS<=cut2, otherwise high. Ties/collapsed cut points may produce empty groups; retain NA rather than move thresholds.

For each group report n, mean new-minus-reference pinball difference and HAC SE. Preserve the full original issue axis. With p_g=n_g/n and mean mu_g, use influence_tg=1{state_t=g}(d_t-mu_g)/p_g on that full axis, and sqrt(HAC(influence_g)/n). Do not compress nonconsecutive group dates. Report a separate two-restriction Wald diagnostic of equal group means using the corresponding full-axis influence covariance; empty groups or singular covariance yield NA.

Implement a Giacomini–White-style finite-instrument multistep moment test, not a proof of the full conditional null. Instruments at issue t: [1, d_(t-H)/a0, RMS_t/v0]. The lagged loss is used only after its H-record response matured. Restrict this diagnostic to the predeclared suffix first_evaluation+H..last (burn-in H rows reported explicitly); all overall and state summaries retain the complete original evaluation. a0 is the original positive response scale from the initial mature training pool; v0 is max(mean(training short RMS),1e-12). There is no evaluation-fitted normalizer. Moments are instruments*(d_t/a0). Use the common centered Bartlett HAC bandwidth and a3-df Wald working reference. Require finite moments, full instrument/covariance rank, positive eigenvalues and condition number<=1e12; otherwise NA with no dropped instrument.

The original GW2006 §3.3 assumes limited-memory methods, moment/mixing conditions and positive long-run covariance. Those assumptions have not been validated here, and the chosen HAC is an exploratory convention. Including the intercept means rejection can reflect only an unconditional mean difference; it does not by itself show volatility dependence. Group heterogeneity and joint equal-accuracy diagnostics are distinct. Four ranking-test slots:2 sources × {joint moments,group-mean heterogeneity}; preserve NA slots in the WP4 family. No state-conditioned forecast fitting, extra regime menu or policy backtest.

Tests require prefix-only RMS/thresholds, known delayed-lag alignment, noncompressed group-axis covariance, a constant mean-advantage example without state effects, and singular/empty-group NA. Actual signs, reversals and nulls determine the descriptive decision-map update.
