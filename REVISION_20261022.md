# Conditional-baseline and independent-source revision — in progress

This substantive revision challenges the existing working paper with direct same-target conditional forecasts and a separate official financial position series. The21October paper and its accepted results remain historical evidence. New financial evaluation is pending; the files added at this milestone do not establish improved accuracy, external validation or a completed revised manuscript.

The question is whether the earlier historical headroom/reanchoring results survive richer conditioning on lagged positions, recent scale and calendar information. The fixed menu adds regularized linear headroom quantiles, multivariate local headroom quantiles and scale-adjusted local quantiles. Original leading rolling rules are retained, with additional same-window/same-refit historical controls. Every declared candidate, numerical failure and adverse complete comparison must be retained. Neither these mature estimators nor a changed dataset is a new algorithm.

The additional source is the New York Fed's weekly primary-dealer Treasury-bills net-position series `PDPOSGS-B`, a separate official compilation in USD millions. It supports a separately defined aggregate-position application of the sampled absolute-peak functional. It does not supply a second hourly FXdealer, disjoint institutions, gross exposure, observed funding need or live public-information forecasts. The downloaded revised snapshot is indexed by its economic as-of dates; public release follows later. Raw source observations and third-party paper PDFs are excluded from publication.
